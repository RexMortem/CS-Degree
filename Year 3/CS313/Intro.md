
## What is a robot?

Widely contested definition. 

Generally though, robots will have sensors (inputs), a "brain", and locomotion/signalling (outputs) - if physical, they will require an energy source.
## Recommended Reading

- Sebastian Thrun, Wolfram Burgard, Dieter Fox, _**Probabilistic Robotics**_, MIT Press, 2005.
- Siegwest and Nourbakhsh, _**Introduction to Autonomous Mobile Robots**_, MIT Press, 2004.
