## Probabilistic Methods

More robust when uncertainty (think complex and unstructured environments). Unfortunately, this is usually computational complex and also an approximate. 

## Proprioceptive vs Exteroceptive

**Proprioceptive:** Internal sensing i.e. sensing the robot
**Exteroceptive:** External sensing i.e. sensing the environment
## Active vs Passive

**Passive:** Sensor just listens and energy comes from the environment.
**Active:** Sensor emits energy and measures the reaction from the environment. 

| Passive              | Active                                                                     |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Doesn't need battery | Needs battery as it consumes energy                                        |
| No interference      | Interference may occur between sensors or otherwise impact the environment |

## Systematic vs Random Errors

**Systematic Error:** that is consistent and repeatable e.g. due to calibration of equipment
**Solution:** Calibration

**Random Error:** that varies between measurement (noise)
**Solution:** Probabilistic Methods
