Brain consists of billions of **neurons** connected to each other via **synapses** - this is a network of neurons or a neural network.

The brain is a Biological Neural Network (BNN) that we mimic with Artificial Neural Networks (ANNs) - there are connections between parts of an ANN and parts of a BNN e.g. dendrites are analogous to inputs.

$\text{Deep Learning} \subseteq \text{Neural Computing} \subseteq \text{Machine Learning} \subseteq \text{AI}$

These relationships are further explored in [[Overview of AI]].
